Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

1. Collection of Personal Information

Nissei ("we", "our inn") may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and phone number when you use our contact form or make a reservation.

Our contact form uses Formspree (Formspree, Inc.). Submitted information is delivered to us via Formspree's servers.

2. Purpose of Use

We use collected personal information only for the following purposes:

  • Guidance and communication regarding reservations and stays
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Statistical analysis to improve our services (in non-identifiable form)

3. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except as required by law. We may share information with contractors (e.g. reservation system providers) to the extent necessary to provide our services.

4. Management of Personal Information

We take appropriate security measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of personal information. Access is limited to staff who need it for business purposes.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This site may use browser local storage for convenience such as language preferences. These do not collect your personal information.

6. Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion

If you wish to disclose, correct, or delete your personal information, please contact us below. We will respond within a reasonable period.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as needed. The revised policy takes effect when posted on this page.
