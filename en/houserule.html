House Rules for Guests

・No smoking indoors

Smoking is not permitted indoors. Please use the designated outdoor smoking area.

・Please keep quiet after 7:00 PM

This facility is in a fishing village where locals head out to sea around 3–4 AM. Please be mindful of footsteps, conversation, and TV volume in the evening.

・No parties or events

To ensure a peaceful stay for other guests and neighbors, we do not accept bookings for parties or events.

・Registered guests only

For safety and security, only guests registered in advance may enter the property.

・No pets

We regret that pets are not allowed, to ensure comfort for guests with allergies and future visitors.

・Open flames

For safety, open flames may only be used in designated areas.

・Trash separation

Please separate trash according to local rules.

・Facilities and furnishings

Please treat all facilities and furnishings with care. Repair or replacement costs may be charged for damage or excessive soiling.
