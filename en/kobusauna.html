KOBU SAUNA

Kobu Sauna

Find your calm overlooking Oshirabetsu's kombu beach

Between sea and forest.
A sauna to "return slowly" — like dried kombu.

KOBU SAUNA was inspired by the sea of Oshirabetsu and the culture of kombu drying.
Warm up thoroughly and find your calm in silence.

Precautions

  • Please refrain from using the sauna if you feel unwell or have consumed alcohol.
  • Those with pre-existing medical conditions should consult a doctor before use.
  • Please proceed at your own pace inside the sauna room.
  • Stay hydrated throughout your session.
  • Please manage your own valuables.
  • We cannot be held responsible for accidents, injuries, or theft on the premises.
  • Please use the facility in a manner consistent with public order and decency.

Usage Guidelines

  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your session begins.
  • To keep the cold bath comfortable for all guests, please shower before entering.
  • This facility does not accept members or associates of organized crime groups.
